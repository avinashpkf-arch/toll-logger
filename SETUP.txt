ONE-TIME CLOUD SETUP (do this once, on a computer or one phone, with your Google account)

1. Google Drive > New > Google Sheets. Name it "Toll Audit Live".
2. In the sheet: Extensions > Apps Script. Delete everything there, paste the contents of Code.gs, click Save.
   Then turn on Google's plate reader: left side "Services" (+) > "Drive API" > Add.
3. Click Deploy > New deployment > gear icon > Web app.
     Execute as:        Me
     Who has access:    Anyone
   Click Deploy. When asked, Authorize access (Advanced > "Go to project (unsafe)" > Allow - this is your own script).
4. Copy the "Web app URL" (ends with /exec).
5. On BOTH phones: open https://avinashpkf-arch.github.io/toll-logger/ in Chrome (it opens on the live camera). Go to the Setup tab,
   paste the URL > Save & test. Then on one phone tap "Side A", on the other tap "Side B".
   Side A starts on ENTRY, Side B on EXIT (you can still switch at the top).

ALREADY DEPLOYED AN OLDER Code.gs?
   Paste the new Code.gs, Save. Add Services (+) > Drive API if it isn't listed. Then Deploy > Manage deployments > pencil icon > Version: "New version" > Deploy.
   This keeps the same URL. ("New deployment" would give you a new URL.)
   If Google asks to authorize again (Drive and Docs access for the plate reader), allow it.

Using it
- Zoom: tap 2x / 3x / 5x (or pinch) until the number plate fills most of the yellow box. At 3 m, 2x-3x is usually right.
  Bigger plate in the box = far better reading. 🔦 turns on the torch at night (on phones that support it).
- Each tap takes 3 quick frames and keeps the sharpest. Hold steady for a moment after tapping.
- The last vehicle's photo and plate show at the top-left of the camera. Green = valid plate, yellow = check it, "? not read" = fix in Log.
- The "Google ✓" chip means plates are read by Google's text recognition (much more accurate). Photos are put in
  your own Google Drive for a second and deleted straight away. If it shows "Reader" instead, add the Drive API service.
- The top-right chip shows sync: "Synced ✓", "3 to send", "Sync ✗" (signal dropped - it retries every 10 seconds),
  or "Clock off!" (phone time is more than a minute out - turn on automatic date & time, or exit times won't match).
- Each phone sees both sides' vehicles. Plate corrections, type changes and deletions sync both ways.
- "Match & download Excel" works on either phone once both have synced.
- Every record also appears live in the "Log" tab of the Google Sheet.
- The app needs internet: the Sheet is where both phones meet. Until it is linked, a red bar on the camera screen says so.
- "Clear this phone" wipes this phone and disconnects it; the Sheet keeps its copy. For a new audit, make a new sheet.

Notes
- Anyone who has the URL can read and write the log. Share it only between you two, and delete the deployment
  (Deploy > Manage deployments > Archive) when the audit is finished.
- Plate numbers are personal data; keep the sheet private to the audit team.
- The plate reader loads when the app opens; wait for "Reader ✓" before the first vehicle.
